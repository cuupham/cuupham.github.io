Notes on making room for unfinished ideas

A small place to begin

Some ideas begin with a very small observation: a patch of light on the wall, a quiet street, or a colour that stays with you after the moment has passed.

I wanted to leave a little space for those unfinished thoughts. Writing them down can be enough for today.
